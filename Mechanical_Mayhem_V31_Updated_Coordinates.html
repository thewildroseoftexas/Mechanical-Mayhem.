How to Operate the Cipher Engine

The tower has awakened the machine, but the coordinates remain encrypted.

  1. Steam Index: Examine the ten brass symbols. Each corresponds to one digit. Read clockwise from the arrow.
  2. Electric Alignment: Count the glowing contacts on the sealed main circuit, then observe the current-direction arrow.
  3. Decoder Dial: Use the clockwise or counterclockwise controls to align the index with the electrical clue.
  4. Coordinate Tape: Once aligned, read the decoded North and West digits. Format them using the engraved coordinate guide.
  5. Beware of decoys: Other workshop instruments may be unreliable. Authentic clues bear the twin-power seal ⚙⚡.

How to Play

Tap the ? badge beside a machinery piece to read its riddle. The badge follows its piece when moved. Drag the actual machinery to the green scale and release to stack. The five numbered boxes automatically show pieces physically supported by the scale, from bottom to top. Every released piece rotates, collides, and tumbles under gravity.

Use the clues to decide which pieces belong and in what order. When you have stacked five, press Confirm Tower Order. Keep the correct tower balanced for five seconds to win.
